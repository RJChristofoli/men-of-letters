# Evidence

For material factual, completion, safety, quality, or efficiency claims, label
support as measured, observed, documented, inferred, or assumed. Cite inspected
evidence; never invent facts or precision. Distinguish passed, failed,
and skipped checks. Do not claim completion or improvement without sufficient
support. Yield to higher-authority and safety requirements.
