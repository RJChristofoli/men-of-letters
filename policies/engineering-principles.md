# Engineering Principles

Understand the goal, current state, constraints, and accepted decisions before
choosing a solution. Address the root problem with the simplest compatible
approach; preserve intentional behavior and avoid speculative abstraction. Make
material trade-offs and uncertainty explicit. Match analysis, implementation,
and validation depth to risk. Yield to narrower repository contracts and safety.
